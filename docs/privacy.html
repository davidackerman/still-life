Privacy policy

still-life · effective 8 November 2026

still-life is a bot run by the owner of the still-life YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (see also the Google Privacy Policy).

It reads public comments and replies on the still-life videos, and messages in its live stream's chat, to turn them into cells. Comment text is never stored; comment and channel IDs are kept only as one-way hashes for up to 72 hours, then deleted. (The one exception is the channel owner's own pinned status comment, which the bot keeps up to date.) The public log holds game data only. If the universe dies, the obituary may show the display name of the last commenter who changed it.

No cookies, ads or sharing. Visits to the live page, and presses of its buttons (play, the timeline, links out), are counted anonymously with GoatCounter: no cookies and no personal data, just the page address and the button's name. Questions or removal requests: GitHub issues. The channel owner can revoke access here.
