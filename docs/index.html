still-life

This video dies when its universe repeats itself.

A YouTube video hosts a live universe running Conway's Game of Life: 128×72 cells on a torus, one generation every two minutes, around the clock. The thumbnail shows the grid and the title shows its status.

Every comment on the video injects cells. Left alone, a finite universe must eventually repeat a state. When that happens a 24-hour countdown starts. If no comment breaks the cycle in time, the universe dies. An obituary video is uploaded and the original goes private.

Every comment changes this universe. Some save it. Some kill it.

How to comment

glider x,y [ne|nw|se|sw]a glider (default se)
block x,y · blinker x,y · rpent x,y · acorn x,ya pattern with its top-left corner at (x, y)
cells x,y x,y …up to 25 single cells
anything elsea 5×5 random patch derived from a hash of your comment

x is 0–127 and y is 0–71, from the top-left. Each channel gets one injection per hour.

Verifiable

Every run is recorded in a hash-chained public log, and anyone can replay the whole life from the seed to check it. The source, the rules in full, and the log are on GitHub.
